## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the craft of digital engineering through an architectural, editorial blueprint aesthetic. Designed for a bilingual developer portfolio and freelance platform, it demystifies production software by presenting the interface as an intentional, structural schematic. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of technical drafting, brutalist clarity, and high-modernist wireframing:
- **Structural Blueprint Aesthetic:** Precision hairline geometry, exposed structural guides, and crosshatch image bounding boxes that treat every module as a structural component.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an immutable 8-point rhythmic grid with a 4-point baseline unit for granular alignment.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, centered with `margin` (`2rem`) gutters (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (`1.5rem`) and `gutter` (`1rem`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, fluid width, `margin-sm` (`1rem`) and `gutter-sm` (`1rem`).

### Blueprint Grid Guides
Sections are framed by a 1px solid hairline (`#CED4DA`) boundary. Major landmark containers expose their structural bounding boxes. Outer section padding alternates between `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` vertically, maintaining mathematical vertical rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eliminates physical shadows, blurred drop-shadows, and skeuomorphic blurs. Depth is conveyed strictly through **tonal layering**, **structural stacking**, and **crisp hairline borders**:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#F8F9FA`. Unbordered canvas baseline.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Background tone `#FFFFFF`, enclosed in a 1px hairline border (`#CED4DA`).
- **Level 2 (Active & Hover Focus):** Background tone `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border upgraded to `#212529`. Optional 2px hard offset shadow: `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px 0px #121212`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Floating Overlay / Popover):** Background tone `#FFFFFF`, bound by a 1px solid `#121212` border, anchored by an opaque 4px hard blueprint shadow: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #212529`. No diffuse feathering allowed.
- **Structural Inset / Well:** Background tone `#F1F3F5` or `#E9ECEF` recessed behind a 1px solid `#CED4DA` outline. Used for wireframe code blocks, landmark labels, and technical specification wells.

## Shapes

The geometry of the design system is completely sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corner Radius:** All borders, panels, buttons, badges, chips, and input boxes feature hard `0px` radii.
- **Geometry Logic:** Reflects engineering drawings, technical blueprints, and raw terminal environments.
- **Visual Boundaries:** Elements terminate cleanly at precise pixel coordinates. Diagonal crosshatching terminates cleanly at exact 90-degree internal container corners.
